Does PawMedal work worldwide?
NFC / QR web profiles require a compatible device and connectivity. Finder coverage is separate and depends on the supported network, region and model. Check the selected product’s confirmed compatibility before ordering.
Do I need a subscription?
Subscription terms for PawMedal products and the profile service are awaiting confirmation. Review the selected product’s current terms before purchase.
How long does the battery last?
NFC / QR access on the Tag is distinct from Finder’s powered finding capability. Finder battery duration and replacement details must be confirmed for the selected model.
Is it waterproof?
Water protection depends on the product. A manufacturer-approved rating and care instructions are needed before a waterproof claim can be made.
Can I update my pet’s information?
The profile concept includes owner-managed pet details. Editing, activation and available fields depend on the connected profile service; the displayed Max profile is an illustrative sample.
What if someone finds my pet?
A finder can use the tag’s NFC / QR link to open the pet profile and view the contact information the owner makes available. This creates a way to get in touch; it does not guarantee recovery.
Is PawMedal compatible with Android?
Opening a web profile in an Android browser is different from using an Android finding network. Finder compatibility must be checked for the exact product or variant; do not assume every Finder supports both Apple and Android networks.
